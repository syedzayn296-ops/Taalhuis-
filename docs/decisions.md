# Taalhuis — Decisions

## Product
- Product: Taalhuis Afrikaans Learning Platform.
- Purpose: Provide practical language-learning support for school learners.
- Current focus: Afrikaans school content with future multilingual expansion.

## Commercial
- Target market: Schools, educators and education organisations.
- Primary validation method: Direct school outreach and feedback.
- Product development should follow real educator and learner needs.

## Development Rules
- Accuracy comes before adding new features.
- Pronunciation and language correctness are high priority.
- School feedback must be recorded and converted into specific fixes.
- Do not build features simply because they are technically possible.
- Keep the prototype usable while improving it.

## Zayzana Test
Every meaningful next step should:
1. Make money.
2. Create an owned reusable asset.
3. Make the next opportunity easier.
