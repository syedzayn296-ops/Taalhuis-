# Taalhuis — Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Complete
- Working Taalhuis prototype.
- School outreach conducted.
- Product tracker established.
- Outreach and feedback tracking established.
- Product documentation established.

## Phase 2 — Improve
- Fix Grades 3-7 pronunciation accuracy.
- Add required Grades 3-7 language structures.
- Review Grade 8 tense word order.
- Improve Junior question words and comprehension.
- Review Junior Zulu Phase 5.

## Phase 3 — Validate
- Continue targeted school outreach.
- Gather educator and learner feedback.
- Identify the highest-value improvements.
- Validate willingness to use and pay.

## Phase 4 — Productise
- Create a stronger school-ready version.
- Develop repeatable school onboarding.
- Explore licensing, subscriptions or school packages.
- Expand multilingual capability where commercially justified.

## Rule
Accuracy and real school demand come before feature expansion.
