# Taalhuis — Strategic Reassessment and January 2027 Reset

**Recorded:** 22 October 2026  
**Status:** Strategic review proposed; owner decision pending. This is a planning record, not approval to rebuild the product or begin new outreach.

## Why reassess

Taalhuis has a working prototype and has received useful educator feedback, but the current pattern of building improvements and sending repeated school follow-ups has not yet produced a confirmed, repeatable path from outreach to learner use. School workload around final-year examinations may be contributing to low response rates, but this is a hypothesis, not a confirmed explanation. Silence alone does not establish rejection or product-market fit.

The proposal is to pause repetitive follow-ups to unresponsive contacts, preserve existing relationships, and use the remaining 2026 period to review the proposition and prepare for a focused January 2027 re-engagement.

## Decisions still required

The owner will consider which objective should lead:

1. **Sustainable business first:** establish a credible route to income while creating a useful education product.
2. **School impact first:** get a genuinely useful Afrikaans learning resource into schools, with income potentially following later.

Both may remain goals, but the leading objective will affect the product scope, pilot offer, target decision-makers, evidence requirements and business model. Do not assume this decision has been made.

## Reassess before rebuilding

### 1. Product proposition and first use case
Clarify whether the immediate offer is primarily:
- a learner self-practice tool;
- a teacher-support tool; or
- a structured school learning programme.

The recommendation under consideration is to choose one narrow, demonstrable use case for the first pilot rather than promote every platform feature at once. Do not describe Taalhuis as fully school-ready until evidence supports that claim. Reassess the existing product before deciding whether any major rebuild is necessary.

### 2. Buyer, user and decision-maker
Distinguish the learner who uses the tool, the educator who evaluates its usefulness, and the school leader or other person who can approve a pilot. The next outreach should aim at a specific decision or feedback request, not merely another contact.

### 3. Evidence and quality
Prioritise verification of:
- Afrikaans grammar and pronunciation accuracy;
- usability by learners with realistic levels of teacher assistance;
- educator-perceived usefulness;
- reliability on ordinary school devices; and
- whether educators would use the tool again after a trial.

A small, structured pilot with documented feedback may provide more useful evidence than a larger volume of unanswered emails. Do not claim a pilot or user outcome until it is confirmed.

### 4. Business model
Options to evaluate—not decisions—include:
- a free resource that builds credibility and school access;
- a paid learner/parent product;
- school subscription or licensing; or
- a free entry-level tool leading to a more comprehensive paid offering.

Do not settle pricing or commercial claims before understanding who values the product, who adopts it and who might pay.

## Proposed October 2026–January 2027 sequence

### Late October — Reassess
- Review product scope, target user, buyer, evidence and business objective.
- Consolidate known educator feedback and outreach status.
- Avoid unnecessary feature additions while the proposition is being reviewed.

### November — Validate
- Correct verified quality problems.
- Select one pilot use case and a small set of success measures.
- Seek targeted educator feedback where there is a genuine route to a conversation.

### December — Prepare
- Prepare a reliable, concise demonstration.
- Prepare a one-page pilot proposal, onboarding steps and a simple feedback/usage measure.
- Account for school holidays and individual school calendars; do not assume every school reopens or becomes available on the same date.

### January 2027 — Re-engage
- Re-contact appropriate prospects when their calendars allow.
- Aim to secure a real demonstration and a credible pilot opportunity, then measure actual use and educator feedback.
- Decide whether to refine, expand, commercialise or pivot based on evidence.

**January target under consideration:** progress towards real learner use in a small pilot, rather than merely increasing the contact list.
